The big idea

Better stores, week by week

One owner, two gift basket stores, and a busy season every few weeks. She wants to open her store and see it getting better.

So I built repeatable workflows for the jobs that move the needle in ecommerce: photos, banners and search. Every week the stores look a little more polished.

The problem

What needed attention

Many listings still used older product shots and screenshots that didn't sell the gift. Collection pages had no banners. A lot of product and collection pages were missing the SEO titles and descriptions that help Google and AI shopping tools find them.

With hundreds of products across two stores, fixing it one listing at a time by hand would never catch up with the seasons.

What I built

The work I handle each week

Lifestyle product photos

Weekly batches by collection or season

I write a short scene brief for each product, and Gemini rebuilds the real product into that scene. Blurry source photos go through a repair step first. Every image gets a search-friendly file name and alt text, is set as the first photo on the listing, and is logged.

Collection banners

Seasonal

16:9 banners with the product on one side and room for text on the other. On Iris Lee I added a banner field and a banner page template, so collections without a banner keep their old look.

Listing and SEO rewrites

By collection

Rewritten titles, descriptions and SEO fields to written standards, collection by collection, like 36 cake and cupcake listings in one pass.

Store upkeep and new lines

Ongoing

A new candy section, gift guides, collection FAQs, menu cleanup and merging duplicate listings.

Weekly traffic and sales check

Weekly

Sales, sessions and search traffic pulled straight from Shopify, so we can see what is working.

Proposal boards for calls

Before each call

Simple visual boards for new collections and products, so decisions take one call instead of three.

The result

What it added up to

  • About 679 products across both stores now lead with a new lifestyle photo, including 100% of the holiday and winter collection.
  • 31 collection banners live on Iris Lee, with seasonal swaps.
  • Boston Gift Baskets, Sep and Oct 2026 vs the same months in 2025: +57% sales, +41% orders, +33% store visits and a +12% bigger average order.
  • October alone: +38% sales vs last October, the store's best month since last December.
  • September alone: +88% sales vs last September.
  • The store is now set up for AI shopping search, and orders started arriving from ChatGPT shopping results in October.

Sales compare the same months year over year and cover Boston Gift Baskets only. Iris Lee orders run outside Shopify.
