The idea

Luxury that shows instead of tells

I started from a real job listing for a creative designer at an editorial beauty brand. It named four very different references, so I set myself the challenge of merging them into one brand with one clear point of view.

The rule behind every choice: true luxury shows instead of tells. The word "luxury" never appears anywhere on the brand. The homepage hero has no button at all, and the products are numbered like a gallery catalog, Formula 01 to Formula 06.

  • Palette: charcoal, espresso and obsidian with travertine stone and warm raw gold.
  • Type: a high-contrast serif for headlines with small, widely tracked sans.
  • Voice: calm, technical and a little aloof. Directions are written as "The Ritual."
