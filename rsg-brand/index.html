Ready Study Go Social Kit

A simple look for the Instagram and Facebook posts: 5 colors, 3 fonts, a few doodles, and 8 example posts. Everything here was designed with ChatGPT and Gemini, so you can keep making more in the same style with the prompt at the bottom.

Brand basics

The colors

Navy and white come straight from the logo. Green and yellow are already in the summer and enrollment posts, so the new look feels like RSG, just more consistent.

Logo Navy#264D81Headlines, labels, the dark tile
Notebook Cream#FFF8ECMain background
Sky Blue#CFE8F5Second background, cards
Leaf Green#6BBF3BButtons, checks, handwriting
Sunshine Yellow#FFD23FUnderlines, tape, stars

The mix on any one post: about 60% cream or sky background, 30% navy text, 10% green and yellow pops. Switch the background color from post to post (cream, sky, yellow, navy) so the grid never looks repetitive.

The fonts

All three are free in Canva and Google Fonts.

Headlines
Fredoka Bold

Round, chunky and friendly. Use it for the big line on every post.

Handwritten accent
Caveat Bold

One short line per post, like a teacher's note in the margin. Never for full sentences.

Small text and captions
Nunito ExtraBold

Labels, card text, dates, and the link in bio button.

The house rules

Always

  • Light notebook grid paper behind everything
  • A navy or yellow tab label at the top naming the series
  • One handwritten line, underlined in yellow or green
  • Little doodles: stars, pencil, pi symbol, check marks
  • The logo small in the bottom right corner
  • One clear button at the bottom when there is an ask
  • About 1 in 3 posts uses photos: a collage of real RSG photos or photo-style images made in ChatGPT or Gemini (desks, books, hands writing, kids from behind), framed with the tape, doodles and colors so the feed is not all text

Never

  • Student faces without a signed photo release
  • Student full names
  • More than one ask on a post (donate OR enroll, not both)
  • Colors outside the five above
  • More than 3 fonts
  • AI-made photos captioned as real RSG students or real RSG events

Read it bottom to top, the way it would post. The scholarship launch goes first, then the everyday series take over.

  • Background colors rotate. Cream, sky, yellow and navy never sit next to each other twice, so the grid feels lively without any extra work.
  • Real photos still fit. The middle right tile is a real class photo already on the account. Mix one in every few posts so it feels human.
  • Grid crop is 3:4. Instagram trims the top and bottom of 4:5 posts in the profile grid, so keep the headline in the middle 80% of the design.

Make more

The prompt to reuse

Paste this into ChatGPT or Gemini, attach the RSG logo, and change only the POST part at the end. Keep going in the same chat so every new post stays on brand.

Create an image. You are a senior social media graphic designer. Attached is the logo for Ready Study Go Academy, a warm, neurodiverse affirming afterschool tutoring academy for K to 12 kids in Cerritos, California. Design a polished, scroll stopping Instagram post, 4:5 portrait (1080x1350), that looks like premium designer work, not clip art.

BRAND SYSTEM (use exactly): Navy #264D81 for headlines, Leaf Green #6BBF3B for buttons, Sunshine Yellow #FFD23F for accents and marker underlines, Sky Blue #CFE8F5 and Cream #FFF8EC for backgrounds. Headline font is a chunky rounded sans like Fredoka Bold, accent words in a handwritten marker script like Caveat, small text in Nunito. Motifs: light notebook grid paper texture, a strip of washi tape, small hand drawn doodles (stars, pencil, pi symbol, check mark, sparkles). Place the attached logo small in the bottom right corner. Style: either flat illustration, OR a photo collage of 2 to 3 realistic photos (study desks, open books, hands writing, kids seen from behind) set inside the branded frame with washi tape and doodles. No clear faces.

POST: [series name and background color]. Small tab label at top: [LABEL]. Headline: [headline]. Handwritten accent: [short line]. Center: [what to show]. Bottom green pill button: [ask, or leave out]. Spell every word exactly as written. Generous spacing, nothing crowded.

Tips for the team

  1. Check every word on the image before posting. AI tools sometimes misspell or swap a word. Ask it to redo with "fix the spelling of X" if so.
  2. If one post comes out off brand, say "same exact style as the last one" and regenerate.
  3. For posts with real photos (tutors, the space, student work), make the layout in AI, then drop the real photo in using Canva.
  4. To break up text-heavy weeks, ask for the photo collage style in the prompt. Use AI photos as mood images only. Real students and real events always get real photos.
  5. Batch the whole week in one sitting, then schedule it all in Meta Business Suite so it posts to Instagram and Facebook together.
