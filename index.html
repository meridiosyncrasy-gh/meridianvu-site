About me

Designer first, systems builder now

I've spent 10+ years in design, ecommerce and short-form content, from print production at Ruggable to running my own fashion brand, Yumelixir, which grew to 10,000+ Instagram followers in its first year. My personal TikTok grew to 32,000+ followers in one summer.

These days I turn the repeat work behind a business into AI skills and automations. I come from design, so I care that what these systems make still looks good and reads like a person made it.

I work remote and async, and I'm happiest with brands where looks matter: fashion, beauty, luxury, interior design and gifting. Premium products deserve a store and a team system that feel just as polished.
