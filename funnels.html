How I build a funnel

Three steps, one job each

  1. Grow the list. Start with who the buyer is and what keeps them up at night, then offer something useful for free in exchange for an email.
  2. Make the first sale. Lay out the offer clearly, add an order bump at checkout and a one-time offer right after, so the first sale helps pay for the ads.
  3. Grow the best buyers. Automated emails and simple application pages move happy buyers into coaching and premium programs, with no extra ad spend.

When a funnel stalls, I look for the exact step where people drop off and fix that step. That habit still shapes how I build stores and systems today.
