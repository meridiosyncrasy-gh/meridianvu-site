Executive summary

Executive summary

Recreated from a real client audit. The business name, identifying details and all numbers have been changed to protect client privacy. This report shows my audit approach, not the client's actual data.

The verdict

Search visits fell about 90% from February to October, meaning fewer people are reaching the bakery through search. The rebuilt site has a solid technical foundation, but its former top search page is broken, phone orders are not tracked, and the bakery's online ratings may discourage new customers.

Biggest win

Marigold & Rye appeared in Google's AI-generated answer and on page 1 for cupcake gift box brambleford on Nov 10. That is a promising sign, but it was one check, not proof the bakery appears consistently.

Biggest risk

The bakery's former top search page, /products/brown-butter-cookie-tin, brought in 138 search visits from January to May. It now shows a Not found error instead of sending shoppers to a working page. Search visits also fell from 119 in February to 12 in October, but we cannot yet confirm how much of that decline came from broken pages.

How this score is calculated

Each of the nine areas is scored 0 to 100 from the evidence in this audit. The health score is the plain average, every area weighted equally. An area with no evidence would show "Not assessed" and be left out, never counted as 0. Scores are a judgment from dated evidence, not a tool score.

    Your old best search page now leads to a Not found error

    Why it mattersBefore the July rebuild, the Brown Butter Cookie Tin page brought in more Google visitors than any other product, with 138 search visits from January to May. Today, that address shows a Not found error. Someone following an old Google result or saved link cannot reach the product, and valuable links from other websites may no longer help the bakery as much as they could.

    Fix steps and evidence

    How to fix

    1. Add a 301 redirect (a permanent forward from an old address to a new one) in Shopify under Online Store, Navigation, URL Redirects: send /products/brown-butter-cookie-tin to /collections/cookie-gift-tins.
    2. Do the same for /products/game-day-cookie-box, /pages/menu-2024 and /collections/featured-treats, pointing each one to its closest live page.

    Evidence

    Shopify visits landing on /products/brown-butter-cookie-tin: 29 to 40 per month from Dec 2025 to Jun 2026, 4 in Jul, 1 in Aug. Live check Nov 10: Not found (404), no redirect. The other 3 former search landing pages also return 404.

    Glossary

    Impressions
    How many times a page showed up in Google results, seen or not.
    Clicks and CTR
    Clicks are visits from Google results. CTR (click rate) is clicks divided by impressions.
    Average position
    Where the page ranked on average. 1 to 10 is roughly page 1.
    Branded search
    A search that includes the business name.
    Indexed
    Stored in Google's index, so it is allowed to show in results.
    Sitemap
    The list of pages the site hands to Google.
    Canonical
    A tag that tells Google which copy of a page is the main one.
    404 and redirect
    A 404 is a missing page. A 301 redirect forwards an old address to a new one.
    Structured data
    Hidden code labels (schema) that describe price, stock, shipping, reviews and the business.
    Core Web Vitals
    Google's speed checks: LCP (main content load time), INP (tap response) and CLS (layout jumping).
    Field vs lab data
    Field data is real Chrome visitors over 28 days. Lab data is one simulated slow phone test.
    AI Overview and AI Mode
    Google's AI written answers at the top of results, and its chat style search mode.
    GA4
    Google Analytics 4, which records visits and, when set up, sales by channel.
    Outbound click
    A Pinterest click that leaves Pinterest and lands on the website.
