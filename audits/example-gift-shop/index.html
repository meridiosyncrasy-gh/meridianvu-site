The verdict

Shopify credits sales to search and AI tools, but missing Google Analytics access makes the full picture hard to check. The biggest opportunities are to finish the Thanksgiving collection, strengthen reviews and get reliable search reporting in place.

Biggest win

Shopify credits 6 orders and about $494 to ChatGPT since Sep 15. The store also ranked #1 on Google for gift baskets port wrenfield and was cited first in Google's AI-generated summary for port wrenfield gift delivery when checked on the audit date.

Biggest risk

Glossary

Impressions
How many times a page showed up in Google results, seen or not.
Clicks and CTR
Clicks are visits from Google results. CTR (click rate) is clicks divided by impressions.
Average position
Where the page ranked on average. 1 to 10 is roughly page 1.
Branded search
A search that includes the business name.
Indexed
Stored in Google's index, so it is allowed to show in results.
Sitemap
The list of pages the site hands to Google.
Canonical
A tag that tells Google which copy of a page is the main one.
404 and redirect
A 404 is a missing page. A 301 redirect forwards an old address to a new one.
Structured data
Hidden code labels (schema) that describe price, stock, shipping, reviews and the business.
Core Web Vitals
Google's speed checks: LCP (main content load time), INP (tap response) and CLS (layout jumping).
Field vs lab data
Field data is real Chrome visitors over 28 days. Lab data is one simulated slow phone test.
AI Overview and AI Mode
Google's AI written answers at the top of results, and its chat style search mode.
GA4
Google Analytics 4, which records visits and, when set up, sales by channel.
Outbound click
A Pinterest click that leaves Pinterest and lands on the website.
