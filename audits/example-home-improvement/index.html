Executive summary

Recreated from a real client audit. The business name, identifying details and all numbers have been changed to protect client privacy. This report shows my audit approach, not the client's actual data.

The verdict

Google brings in around 1,300 clicks a month, mostly through product pages. But unclear page titles, broken links and missing sales tracking are costing the store opportunities. With 518K search appearances, Juniper & Stone Home has room to earn more visits and sales.

Biggest win

Google showed the vanity finish sample and backsplash tile pages about 63,000 times in 90 days, often on the first page of results. Almost nobody clicked. Clearer titles could turn some of those missed clicks into shoppers.

Biggest risk

Google Analytics is not recording purchases, even though Shopify shows orders. That means the store cannot reliably see which Google searches, Pinterest pins or AI tools lead to sales. Marketing decisions are being made without the full picture.

This report is based on real client work, with business details, numbers and findings recreated for privacy. It does not show the client's actual results. Client audits use their own data from the sources listed.

This was a read only audit: nothing was changed in the store, Search Console, GA4 or Pinterest while it ran. I pulled every number from the source named next to it, for the date range shown, and checked pages directly with a crawl and PageSpeed Insights. All reported performance figures come from the named sources and periods. The health score is Meridian Vu's assessment of the evidence, and the 90 day targets are planning goals, not measured results or guarantees. Where a source could not be reached, the report says so instead of filling the gap.

Evidence labels

  • Official Google, Bing or Pinterest documentation says so.
  • Secondary An industry source reports it; not confirmed by the platform.
  • Observed Seen directly in this audit's data or checks.

Scoring

Nine areas scored 0 to 100 from the evidence. The health score is their plain average (see Executive summary). Bands: 70 and up Good, 40 to 69 Needs work, under 40 Urgent. Severity: Severe means losing sales or pages now, High means big upside within 30 days, Moderate means days 31 to 90, Long term means builds over time.

Glossary

Impressions
How many times a page showed up in Google results, seen or not.
Clicks and CTR
Clicks are visits from Google results. CTR (click rate) is clicks divided by impressions.
Average position
Where the page ranked on average. 1 to 10 is roughly page 1.
Branded search
A search that includes the business name.
Indexed
Stored in Google's index, so it is allowed to show in results.
Sitemap
The list of pages the site hands to Google.
Canonical
A tag that tells Google which copy of a page is the main one.
404 and redirect
A 404 is a missing page. A 301 redirect forwards an old address to a new one.
Structured data
Hidden code labels (schema) that describe price, stock, shipping, reviews and the business.
Core Web Vitals
Google's speed checks: LCP (main content load time), INP (tap response) and CLS (layout jumping).
Field vs lab data
Field data is real Chrome visitors over 28 days. Lab data is one simulated slow phone test.
AI Overview and AI Mode
Google's AI written answers at the top of results, and its chat style search mode.
GA4
Google Analytics 4, which records visits and, when set up, sales by channel.
Outbound click
A Pinterest click that leaves Pinterest and lands on the website.
