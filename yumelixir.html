Yumelixir

Yumelixir is my own fashion label, known for its pastel, playful look. As its creative director I handle the product design, the photoshoots, the modeling, the social content and the print pieces. In its first year it grew to 10,000+ Instagram followers. Running my own brand taught me what it takes to keep a store and its content consistent.
