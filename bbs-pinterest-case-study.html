I turned plain product photos into on-model lifestyle shots in places the boutique's customer knows: city errands, the farmers market and weekend plans. Drag each slider to compare. Each one went to the boutique for approval before it ran.

The system

How I ran Pinterest
across accounts

  • Audited 12 boutique Pinterest accounts using organic, ads, audience and conversion data, and turned the findings into a plan to improve the agency's Pinterest service.
  • Wrote a 2026 trend guide for each boutique that translated Pinterest's own trend report into that shop's customer, inventory and wording, with what to say and what to avoid.
  • Built Pinterest strategy and content calendars for client boutiques, plus a profile refresh with new headers and bios.
  • Wrote team instructions for pin content and product image prompts, so every account followed the same standard.

The same steps work for any shop: find what the data says is working, plan around what shoppers are already searching for, and make creative that looks like the brand on its best day.
