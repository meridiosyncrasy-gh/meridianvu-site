Blush
Reverie Studio

A Shopify shop for pink home decor, vanities and printable wall art, made for renters who want a pretty room on a real budget. I built the whole launch kit: the brand, the storefront design, the product photos and a 16 pin Pinterest feed, all in one look.

The goal

A pink shop that looks like one brand

  • A catalog of vanities, mirrors, chairs and decor from different makers, presented as one curated shop.
  • Product photos that show each piece in a real, styled room instead of a white backdrop.
  • A Pinterest launch that brings in shoppers who are already searching for pink room ideas.
  • A storefront that guides a first visit from inspiration to the right product.

Everything grows from the brand kit. The storefront, the photos and the pins all reuse the same palette, type and signature details, so a shopper who taps a pin lands on a page that feels like the pin they just saved.

Brand kit

A soft, romantic look with rules that hold up

A script and serif logo set with a satin bow, a blush and rose palette, and a small set of details like the bow, the scallop edge and pearl dividers that every graphic reuses.

Storefront design

Home, collection and product pages

Shop by room, a free printable to grow the email list, a friendly hello section, and product pages that show sizes and what comes in the set.

blushreveriestudio.com (design)
Product photos

Every product in a styled pink room

Lifestyle photos for the catalog, with each color option getting its own photo. Every image is checked against the maker's photos so the product stays exactly true to what ships.

Pinterest launch feed

Sixteen pins, sixteen different layouts

Guides and size charts that answer real searches, color stories and mood boards that get saved, and shop picks. No two neighbors share a layout or a room.
