The big idea

Better stores, week by week

One owner, a Boston gift basket store, and a busy season every few weeks. She wants to open her store and see it getting better.

So I built repeatable workflows for the jobs that move the needle in ecommerce: photos, banners and search. Every week the store looks a little more polished.

She also owns Iris Lee, a sister bakery on Shopify, where I run the same weekly workflow. A few examples below come from there and are labeled. I also finished Iris Lee's move from WordPress to Shopify.

The problem

Where the store fell short

Many listings still used older product shots and screenshots that didn't sell the gift. Seasonal banners looked dated. A lot of product and collection pages were missing the SEO titles and descriptions that help Google and AI shopping tools find them.

With hundreds of products, fixing it one listing at a time by hand would never catch up with the seasons.

What I built

The work I handle each week

Lifestyle product photos

Weekly batches by collection or season

I write a short scene brief for each product, and Gemini rebuilds the real product into that scene. Blurry source photos go through a repair step first. Every image gets a search-friendly file name and alt text, is set as the first photo on the listing, and is logged.

Collection banners

Seasonal

16:9 banners with the product on one side and room for text on the other, swapped for each gifting season.

Listing and SEO rewrites

By collection

Rewritten titles, descriptions and SEO fields to written standards, collection by collection, like 36 cake and cupcake listings in one pass.

Store upkeep and new lines

Ongoing

A new candy section, gift guides, collection FAQs, menu cleanup and merging duplicate listings.

Weekly traffic and sales check

Weekly

Sales, sessions and search traffic pulled straight from Shopify, so we can see what is working.

Proposal boards for calls

Before each call

Simple visual boards for new collections and products, so decisions take one call instead of three.

The result

What it added up to

  • About 679 products, counting sister store Iris Lee, now lead with a new lifestyle photo, including 100% of the holiday and winter collection.
  • New seasonal banners on Boston Gift Baskets, plus 31 on sister store Iris Lee.
  • 194 product listings rewritten on Boston Gift Baskets, with titles in the words shoppers search, image alt text and clear shipping facts.
  • Boston Gift Baskets, Sep 1 to Nov 7, 2026 vs the same dates in 2025: +70% sales and +54% orders. Sep and Oct alone also brought a +12% bigger average order.
  • September alone: +88% sales and +70% orders vs last September.
  • October alone: +38% sales vs last October, the store's best month since last December.
  • First week of November: 11 orders, up from 2 the same week last year.
  • Store visits, June to October: +39% overall, and +67% from shoppers who typed the address or used a bookmark, so more people are coming back.
  • About 16.67% of orders since September 9 came from ChatGPT or Gemini, with listings now clear enough for AI shopping tools to recommend.
  • Started the store's blog (6 gift guides) and Pinterest, plus abandoned cart emails.

Sales and orders compare the same dates year over year and cover Boston Gift Baskets only.
