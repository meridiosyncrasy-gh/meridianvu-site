How I plan

Every pin has a job

  • A content plan with the searches to answer, the boards, and a mix of guides, know-how, ideas and shop pins.
  • Click pins send people to a guide or product. Savable pins earn saves and reach.
  • No two neighbors share a layout or a scene, and the brand shows once per pin.
  • Every headline is broken by hand and checked word for word before it goes live.
